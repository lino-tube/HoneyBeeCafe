## Project Overview:
 HoneyBee Cafe is a fictional cafe website designed to provide customers with information about the cafe, its products and services, and ways to communicate with the customers.
 
 HoneyBee Cafe offers homemade pastries, light meals, and a variety of hot and cold beverages. The cafe was established in Port Elizabeth in 2018 and began to grow late 2019, particulary amoung students. Over time, the cafe also became popular with tourists.

 The website aims to create a warm and welcoming online presence for HoneyBee Cafe while making it easy for users to explore the menu, learn about the cafe, make enquiries, and find contact and location information.

## Website Goals and Objectives:
 - Increase online visibility, 
 - Attract new and more customers, 
 - Allow customers to make online orders, 
 - Display the café’s products.

## Key Features and Functionality:
### Home
 The home page introduces customers to HoneyBee Cafe and provides an overview of the cafe and what it offers.
### About 
 The about page provides information about the history of HoneyBee Cafe, how the cafe was established, its owners, mission, vision, and members of the cafe team.
### Products & Services
 The products & Services page displays the cafe's products, including the names and prices of the products. Customers can browse the different menu categories and view the products offered by the cafe.
### Contact 
 The contact page provides HoneyBee Cafe's contact information, current location, business hours, and information about the cafe's future expansion. It also includes a contact form and the cafe's map.
### Enquiry 
 The enquiry page allows customers to submit enquiries about HoneyBee Cafe. Customers can make product and menu enquiries, and order enquiries.

### Enquiry Form Functionality

The Enquiry form provides different fields according to the enquiry type selected by the customer:

| Enquiry Type | Information Displayed |
|--------------|-----------------------|
| Product & Menu Enquiry | A message field for questions about menu products. |
| Order Enquiry | Order items, delivery or collection, preferred date and time, and optional additional information. |
| Visit & Availability | A message field for questions about opening days, operating hours and suitable visiting times. |
| General Cafe Enquiry | A message field for general enquiries. |

For order enquiries, the delivery address becomes visible and required only if the customer selects **Delivery**. The form checks the relevant information before sending it to Formspree. Following an accepted submission, it displays a personalised message, clears the fields and returns to the initial enquiry selection.

### Contact Form Functionality

The Contact form collects the customer's full name, email address, phone number, message type and message. JavaScript checks the entered information and displays helpful errors when necessary. After Formspree accepts a valid submission, the page displays a personalised confirmation and clears the form (Mozilla, n.d.-b; Formspree, 2026).

### Search Engine Optimisation

The website includes page-specific titles and meta descriptions, descriptive image alternative text, internal navigation links, responsive styling, an XML sitemap and a robots file. The `sitemap.xml` file identifies the five published website pages, and `robots.txt` tells crawlers where to find the sitemap (Google, n.d.-a; Google, n.d.-c). Alternative text provides text descriptions of meaningful images (World Wide Web Consortium, n.d.).
