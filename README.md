## Project Overview:
 HoneyBee Cafe is a fictional cafe website designed to provide customers with information about the cafe, its products and services, and ways to communicate with the customers.
 
 HoneyBee Cafe offers homemade pastries, light meals, and a variety of hot and cold beverages. The cafe was established in Port Elizabeth in 2018 and began to grow late 2019, particulary amoung students. Over time, the cafe also became popular with tourists.

 The website aims to create a warm and welcoming online presence for HoneyBee Cafe while making it easy for users to explore the menu, learn about the cafe, make enquiries, and find contact and location information.

## Website Goals and Objectives:
 - Increase online visibility, 
 - Attract new and more customers, 
 - Allow customers to make online orders, 
 - Display the café’s products.

## Key Features and Functionality:
### Home
 The home page introduces customers to HoneyBee Cafe and provides an overview of the cafe and what it offers.
### About 
 The about page provides information about the history of HoneyBee Cafe, how the cafe was established, its owners, mission, vision, and members of the cafe team.
### Products & Services
 The products & Services page displays the cafe's products, including the names and prices of the products. Customers can browse the different menu categories and view the products offered by the cafe.
### Contact 
 The contact page provides HoneyBee Cafe's contact information, current location, business hours, and information about the cafe's future expansion. It also includes a contact form and the cafe's map.
### Enquiry 
 The enquiry page allows customers to submit enquiries about HoneyBee Cafe. Customers can make product and menu enquiries, and order enquiries.

## Part 1 - HTML Development:
 Part 1 focuses on developing the structure and content of the HoneyBee Cafe website using HTML.
 
 The following pages were created:
 ### Home Page
  The home page was created to introduce users to HoneyBee Cafe. It includes the cafe's logo, navigation menu, hero image, welcome section, cafe slogan, and sections introducing the main categories of the cafe's products and services.
  ### Products & Services Page
   The products & services page contains the cafe's menu, which is divided into three main categories:
   - Drinks
   - Light Meals
   - Pastries
  Each menu item includes its name, image and price
 ### Enquiry Page
  An enquiry page was created to allow customers to submit different types of enquires.
  
  The enquiry form includes options for:
  - Product & Menu Enquiry
  - Order Enquiry
  - Visit & Availability
  - General Cafe Enquiry

 The form also allows customers to provide their personal details and submit their enquiry.
 For order enquiries, customers can indicate whether they would prefer delivery or to collect their order from the cafe.
### About Page
 The about page was created to provide information about the cafe and its background.

 It includes:
 - The history and story of HoneyBee Cafe
 - The cafe's mission
 - The cafe's vision
 - Information about the founders and owners
 - Information about the cafe's kitchen and bakery team
 - Information about the cafe's baristas and cafe servers.
### Contact Page
 The contact page was created to provide customers with ways to contact honeybee Cafe.

 It includes:
 - Contact information
 - The cafe's current location
 - Future expansion plans
 - Business hours
 - A contact form
 - The map of the cafe
