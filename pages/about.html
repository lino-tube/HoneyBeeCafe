About HoneyBee Cafe

History of HoneyBee Cafe

HoneyBee Cafe opened its doors in 2018 in Port Elizabeth, offering customers homemade pastries, light meals, and a variety of hot and cold drinks.

The beginning was not easy, and the cafe experienced a rocky start. However, by late 2019, HoneyBee Cafe began to bloom and became popular especially among students

Over the years, the cafe became increasingly popular with tourists as well. This allowed HoneyBee Cafe to grow its customer base and become a well-known cafe.

Today, HoneyBee Cafe continues to focus on providing amazing, homey and high-quality pastries together with tasty food and beverages.


Our Mission

Our mission is to bake amazing, homey and high-quality pastries, along with tasty food and beverages. We aim to make our customers feel at home through our food and the warm experience we provide.


Our Vision

Our vision is to grow HoneyBee Cafe by opening other cafes in cities such as East London. We aim to become well-known across the Eastern Cape, and eventually across South Africa and other countries for our homemade pastries.


Meet Our Founders/Owners

Lisa Brown

Lisa is the co-owner and baker of HoneyBee Cafe. She oversees the bakery and is responsible for creating and maintaining the quality of the cafe's homemade pastries and baked goods.

Heather Smith

Heather is the co-owner and chef of HoneyBee Cafe. She oversees the preparation of the cafe's food while also managing the financial side of the business.


HoneyBee Cafe began as a dream shared by two best friends while they were still in college. One was pursuing a passion for baking, while the other was working towards becoming a chef.

At the time, owning a cafe seemed almost impossible. It was a dream they continued to write about, plan for and hope would one day become a reality. Eventually, they secured the funds needed to bring their dream to life.

When HoneyBee Cafe opened its doors in 2018, it marked the beginning of a new chapter for the founders and their vision. What began as a dream between two best friends eventually became the HoneyBee Cafe enjoyed by students, locals and tourists today.
